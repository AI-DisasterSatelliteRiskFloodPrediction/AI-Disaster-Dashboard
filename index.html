Featured Portfolio Project

AI Disaster Dashboard

Project Research Theme

Integrating AI-Driven Satellite Image Analysis into National Disaster Risk Reduction Strategies

The AI Disaster Dashboard is a portfolio project exploring how satellite image analysis, geospatial data, and AI-assisted workflows could support flood susceptibility assessment, climate resilience, and disaster risk reduction in Rwanda and other emerging space nations.

Project Objectives

  • Explore the potential of satellite observations for environmental and disaster-related analysis.
  • Investigate geospatial workflows for mapping areas that may be susceptible to flooding.
  • Demonstrate how web mapping can help communicate geographic information.
  • Explore how data-driven evidence could inform disaster preparedness and planning.
