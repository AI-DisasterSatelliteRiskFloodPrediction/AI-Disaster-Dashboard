About the researcher

About ISHIMWE Serge

ISHIMWE Serge is the creator of the AI Disaster Risk Dashboard, a project exploring the use of Earth observation, Google Earth Engine, geospatial technology, and artificial intelligence for flood risk monitoring in Rwanda.

This portfolio presents the project's goals, research workflow, interactive map prototype, and potential applications for disaster risk reduction and climate resilience.

The project aims to support exploration and research. Any operational flood-risk assessment requires appropriate data, validation, and expert review.

Research methodology

Proposed workflow

A transparent workflow for developing and validating a geospatial risk-monitoring tool.

  1. Data collection
    Identify suitable satellite, rainfall, terrain, and reference datasets.
  2. Preprocessing
    Prepare imagery and spatial data using documented methods.
  3. Feature analysis
    Investigate environmental variables relevant to flood hazards.
  1. Model development
    Evaluate an appropriate analytical or machine-learning approach.
  2. Validation
    Compare outputs with reliable reference observations and report uncertainty.
  3. Communication
    Present validated results, limitations, and data sources in the dashboard.
